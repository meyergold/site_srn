<p class="srn-eyebrow">Setup</p>

A Sairen agent doesn't improvise: it <span class="srn-g">applies your rules</span>. The sharper its framing, the more reliable it is. Follow these six steps to put it to work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the role and goals">
    Describe who the agent is and what it must achieve: its mission, its scope, the expected outcomes. A clear role prevents off-topic answers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give it knowledge">
    Feed its knowledge base: documents, FAQs, procedures, catalog. The agent relies only on these sources — it invents nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the data to collect">
    Specify the information to capture in every exchange (identity, reason, contact details…). It flows automatically into the unified CRM record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the voice and tone">
    Tune the voice, pace and tone to reflect your brand. The same business rule, expressed in your style.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure calls">
    Set the conversational behavior: interruption handling, voicemail detection, transfers, end-of-call actions (SMS, appointments, human escalation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Analyze and iterate">
    Track performance, review transcripts and adjust the rules. Every iteration makes the agent more accurate.
  </Step>
</Steps>
