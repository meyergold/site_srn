Connect Pipedrive so your teams and your AI agents work on <span class="srn-g">a single customer record</span>: conversations handled in Sairen feed your CRM, and your Pipedrive data informs every exchange — no double entry.

## What the integration syncs

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contacts" icon="address-card">
    Contact records reconciled between Pipedrive and the Sairen CRM, both ways.
  </Card>

  <Card title="History" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Conversations, calls and data collected by your AI agents flow back to the Pipedrive record.
  </Card>

## Connect Pipedrive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Pipedrive">
    Connect your Pipedrive account and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the data">
    Select the objects to sync (contacts, history) and the sync direction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases

- **Lead qualification**: your AI agents qualify inbound requests 24/7 and create or enrich the contact in Pipedrive.
- **Sales follow-up**: trigger cross-channel re-engagement campaigns on your segments, with results tracked in the CRM.
