Connect Zendesk so your teams and your AI agents work on <span class="srn-g">a single customer record</span>: requests handled in Sairen stay visible in Zendesk, and the Zendesk history informs every exchange — no double entry.

## What the integration syncs

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Contacts" icon="address-card">
    Customer records reconciled between Zendesk and the Sairen CRM, both ways.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Requests and history" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Conversations and data collected by your AI agents flow back to the Zendesk record.
  </Card>

## Connect Zendesk

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Zendesk">
    Connect your Zendesk account and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the data">
    Select the objects to sync (contacts, requests) and the sync direction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use cases

- **24/7 support**: your AI agents answer routine requests at any hour and escalate to a human when needed.
- **Full context**: request history is visible on the unified record, for sharper answers on every channel.
