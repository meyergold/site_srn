## What the integration enables

## Connect WhatsApp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your WhatsApp Business number">
    Connect your WhatsApp Business account or create your number with us.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the scenarios">
    AI-agent replies, campaigns and notifications following your business rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>
