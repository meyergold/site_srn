<p class="srn-eyebrow">Communication</p>

Enable the SMS channel to reach your customers <span class="srn-g">where they answer most</span>: appointment reminders, confirmations, campaigns and two-way conversations, handled by your teams and your AI agents.

## What the channel enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SMS conversations" icon="comment-sms">
    Inbound SMS land in the Océan inbox, qualified and attached to the contact record.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Anti no-show reminders" icon="bell">
    Automatic confirmations and reminders before every appointment, with reply support.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Outbound campaigns" icon="paper-plane">
    Targeted SMS campaigns on your segments, coordinated with your other channels.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Call follow-ups" icon="phone">
    Your AI agents send an SMS at the end of an exchange: payment link, summary, appointment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Enable SMS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your number">
    Use a number provided by Sairen or port your existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the scenarios">
    Reminders, campaigns and end-of-call actions following your business rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>
