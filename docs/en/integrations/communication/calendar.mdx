<p class="srn-eyebrow">Communication</p>

Connect your calendars so your AI agents <span class="srn-g">book appointments for you</span>: real-time slot suggestions, instant booking by phone or in writing, automatic confirmations and reminders.

## What the integration enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Appointment booking" icon="calendar-check">
    Your AI agents check availability and book directly in your calendar, 24/7.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Anti no-show reminders" icon="bell">
    Automatic confirmations and reminders by SMS, WhatsApp or email before every appointment.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Rescheduling" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Cancellations and changes handled in conversation — the calendar stays up to date.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Workflow triggers" icon="bolt">
    A calendar event can trigger your Rainbow automations (follow-up, task, reminder).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect your calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your calendar">
    Connect your calendar (Google, Outlook or booking tool) and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the rules">
    Open slots, durations, buffers and reminder scenarios to match your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>
