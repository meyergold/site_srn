<p class="srn-eyebrow">Communication</p>

Connect Gmail so your email lands in <span class="srn-g">the same inbox as every other channel</span>: each message is qualified automatically (priority, sentiment, intent), attached to the contact record, and handled by your teams or your AI agents.

## What the integration enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Centralized email" icon="inbox">
    Your Gmail inboxes join Océan, alongside phone, SMS and WhatsApp.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI-agent replies" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Your AI agents answer routine requests 24/7, following your rules, and escalate when needed.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Unified contact record" icon="user">
    Every email exchange is added to the contact's omnichannel history.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Email campaigns" icon="paper-plane">
    Email plugs into your outbound campaigns and cross-channel re-engagement scenarios.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect Gmail

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Google">
    Connect the Google account behind your inbox and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the inboxes">
    Select the addresses to centralize and the handling rules.
  </Step>
</Steps>
