<p class="srn-eyebrow">Communication</p>

Connect Microsoft Teams to keep your teams informed <span class="srn-g">without switching tools</span>: Sairen's important events (requests to handle, AI-agent escalations, alerts) surface in your Teams channels.

## What the integration enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Team notifications" icon="bell">
    Escalations, urgent requests and alerts surface in the Teams channels of your choice.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Smooth collaboration" icon="users">
    Your teams jump from Teams to the relevant Sairen conversation in one click.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Connected workflows" icon="bolt">
    Your Rainbow automations can notify Teams at every key step.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Unified history" icon="user">
    Handling stays tracked in Sairen — the contact record keeps the full history.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect Teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Microsoft">
    Connect your Microsoft 365 account and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the channels">
    Select the Teams channels to notify and the events involved.
  </Step>
</Steps>
