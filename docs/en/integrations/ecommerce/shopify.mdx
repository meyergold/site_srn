## Connect Shopify

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate your store">
    Connect your Shopify store and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the data">
    Select the objects to sync (customers, orders) and enable your scenarios.
  </Step>
</Steps>
