Connect Klaviyo to link <span class="srn-g">your marketing to your conversations</span>: data collected by your teams and your AI agents enriches your segments, and your campaigns run on an up-to-date customer record.

## What the integration enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Synced segments" icon="users">
    Contacts and attributes collected in Sairen feed your Klaviyo segments.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Connected campaigns" icon="paper-plane">
    Your campaigns work with Sairen channels (email, SMS, WhatsApp) and cross-channel re-engagement.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Unified customer record" icon="user">
    Marketing history informs every conversation — replies account for the campaigns received.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Conversion loop" icon="arrows-rotate">
    Cart recovery and repeat purchase coordinated between Klaviyo and Sairen AI agents.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect Klaviyo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate Klaviyo">
    Connect your Klaviyo account and grant access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the flows">
    Select the segments and events to sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>
