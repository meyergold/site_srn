Connect Doctolib so your AI agents handle <span class="srn-g">appointment booking end to end</span>: by phone or in writing, 24/7, with automatic reminders — and a unified patient history, hosted in France (HDS).

## What the integration enables

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Appointment booking" icon="calendar-check">
    Your AI agents offer slots and book directly in Doctolib, at any hour.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Anti no-show reminders" icon="bell">
    Automatic confirmations and reminders by SMS, WhatsApp or email before every appointment.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Workflow triggers" icon="bolt">
    A Doctolib event (appointment created, cancelled…) can trigger your Rainbow automations.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Unified patient record" icon="user">
    Every interaction feeds the patient record (PRM) — full history, no double entry.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connect Doctolib

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open integrations">
    From your Sairen workspace, open the Integrations section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link your Doctolib calendar">
    Connect your account and choose the calendars involved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the scenarios">
    Appointment booking by your AI agents, reminders, and workflow triggers.
  </Step>
</Steps>
